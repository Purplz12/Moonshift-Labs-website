// ABOUT

WHO IS MOONSHIFT?

A small independent game studio working on a bunch of strange ideas.

Moonshift Labs is an independent game development studio. We make games across different genres, with a particular interest in VR, multiplayer experiences, horror, and weird experimental concepts.

Some projects start as a random idea and turn into something much bigger. Others are made simply because they sounded fun. Either way, we're here to make games that have their own personality.

MADE IN THE LAB

Game development, 3D art, environments, multiplayer systems, VR mechanics, monsters, and whatever else a project ends up needing.
